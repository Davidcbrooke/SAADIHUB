Set-up and the facts Claude uses

Facts Claude uses

Claude only answers from these facts. Keep them up to date (term dates, class times, prices you're happy to quote, how to book). If something isn't here, Claude says reception will check and flags it for a person.

Switching it on (once)


  1. In Chrome signed in as reception@, open script.google.com and open SAADI Enquiry Desk mailer. Click in the code, press Ctrl+A then Ctrl+V, and press Save.
  2. Choose allowSending next to Run and press Run. Google asks for one new permission (to reach the Hub): Review permissions > reception@ > Advanced > Go to SAADI Enquiry Desk mailer > Allow.
  3. Deploy > Manage deployments, click the pencil, set Version to New version, then Deploy. The web address stays the same.
  5. Give Claude's hourly job its details:
    Open C:\NGL\credentials\.env in Notepad, paste them at the end, save, and tell Claude "done". (They only open the Email assistant's own section of the Hub: not the Enquiry Desk, renewals or anything else, and they can't send email.)
