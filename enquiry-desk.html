☀ Start of day Checking… About 2 minutes, first thing each morning
  1. Update the class numbers from JustGo. The desk doesn't update itself: it shows the spaces from the last time someone pressed Send to SAADI Hub, so do it every morning.
    1. (sign in if JustGo asks).
    2. Click Send to SAADI Hub on the bookmarks bar. Keep the JustGo tab in front until it says All … classes are ready (about 20 seconds).
    3. Click Open SAADI Hub, then Save week (or Replace this week). You'll be brought back here.

    No Send to SAADI Hub button on the bookmarks bar? Press Ctrl+Shift+B to show the bar. If it's still not there, open Class Capacity, click Add this week's numbers and drag the red button onto the bar.

  2. Record yesterday's bookings. In JustGo, check who booked a trial or a place. On the Follow-ups tab, press Trial booked or Booked a place for them, so they don't get chased.
  3. Clear today's follow-ups.
    Press the pink button on each one to send the email. Book-your-place emails go out after the trial has finished.

Sending emails from reception@

Not set up

Do this once, on the reception PC, in Chrome signed in to Google as reception@saadigymnastics.co.uk. It takes about 5 minutes. After that, emails go out from that account on any PC that opens the desk, and copies appear in its Gmail Sent folder.


  1. This also opens a new Google Apps Script project. If Google asks which account to use, choose reception@.
  2. In the new project, select everything in the editor (Ctrl+A), paste (Ctrl+V), then press the Save (disk) button. Name it SAADI Enquiry Desk mailer.
  3. At the top, choose allowSending next to Run and press Run. Click Review permissions, choose reception@, then Advanced > Go to SAADI Enquiry Desk mailer > Allow.
  4. Press Deploy > New deployment, click the cog and choose Web app. Set Execute as: Me and Who has access: Anyone, then press Deploy and copy the Web app URL.
