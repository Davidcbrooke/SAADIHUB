What each page is for

One place for each job. Everything starts from People: change something there and the rota, cover and payroll all follow.

PageUse it to…Who
PeopleEverything about each person: details, contract (40 or 52 weeks), hourly or salaried, band, working days, the groups they coach, holiday left. Add new staff. Approve holiday. Record time off or sickness when someone rings in.Managers
RotaSee everyone's shifts as a calendar. Red = a shift that needs cover. Change who's on a class for one week or for good. Start a new term.Managers
Shift offersEveryone who has said "I can do this" for a shift. Pick one person; the rota updates and everyone's told.Managers
DocumentsSend a contract to sign and counter-sign it. Company documents everyone must read, and who has read them.Managers
PayrollMonth end: hours to pay, holiday, sick days (SSP), extra hours. Download for QuickBooks.Owners / managers
SAADI Team appStaff: their shifts, ask for holiday, "I'm not well", pick up spare shifts, read and sign documents.Everyone
These pages use your own sign-in (your SAADI email and a 6-digit code), not the Hub password. The button top right says Sign out (your name), so you can always see who you're signed in as.

Getting started (once)

1Sign in

  1. Open Team › People.
  2. Type your SAADI work email (Dave: admin@saadigymnastics…, not the NGL one) and click Email me a code.
  3. Type the 6-digit code from the email. You stay signed in on that computer.

2Check each person on People

  1. Click a name. Check Contract (term time 40 weeks / all year 52 weeks), Pay (hourly / salaried) and Band (A–H).
  2. Tick their Working days and times.
  3. Under On the rota, tick the classes they coach. Click Save.

3Get staff onto the app

  1. Send everyone the link hub.saadigymnastics.co.uk/team and the "For staff" steps below.
  2. On People, the App column shows who has signed in.

4Company documents

  1. Open Documents › Company documents. The 18 from Breathe are already there.
  2. When the risk assessments are final, click New version on each and upload the final PDF.
  3. When you're ready for staff to read them, tick Every Monday, email each person… at the bottom.

Every week

!Fill the gaps on the rota

  1. Open Rota. The top row, Needs cover, shows every shift where the person is on holiday or off sick.
  2. Click a red shift. Choose who to offer it to: 1. Same band, 2. One band down or 3. A particular person. It only lists people who are free then.
  3. Add a note if useful and click Send. The shift turns amber; those staff get a phone alert and an email.

✓Choose who covers

  1. You're emailed when someone says yes. Open Shift offers.
  2. Each person shows their band, their hours that week and any clash. Click Give them the shift (or Not this time).
  3. The rota updates straight away, the shift turns green, and everyone's told. Nothing else to do.

☀Holiday requests

  1. Open People. Waiting for you lists requests, with who else is off and a warning if it's within 3 weeks.
  2. Click Approve or Decline. They're told on their phone. Approved holiday shows red on the rota so you can find cover.

✎Changing the rota

  1. On Rota, click any shift (or switch to By class) and click Change on the slot.
  2. Tick who's on, then Just this week or From this week on. Back to the usual undoes a one-off.
  3. Copy changes to next week repeats this week's changes; Make this week the usual makes them permanent.

+Add a shift for someone

  1. On Rota (Calendar), click an empty part of the day in their row (a + shows when you hover). Not on the rota this week? Pick them in the last row, Someone else…, then click the day.
  2. Either tick a class that day, or fill in a shift that isn't a class (e.g. Admin 9am–12pm).
  3. Click Just this week or Every week from now. It counts in their hours and payroll straight away.

When something happens

🤒Someone rings in sick (or asks for time off in person)

  1. People › click their name › Add time off or sickness for them.
  2. Choose Off sick (leave "To" blank if you don't know), Holiday or Other leave, the dates, then Add.
  3. Their shifts go red on the rota: find cover as above.
  4. When they're back: People › Waiting for you › Back at work.
If staff report sickness themselves in the app, you get an alert and it's on the rota already. Only Dave and Mel see what's wrong; managers see dates only. Don't type medical details in notes.

+A new member of staff

  1. People › + Add member of staff.
  2. Fill in their details, contract, pay, band, personal details and emergency contact.
  3. Tick their working days and times. Tick the classes under On the rota (empty ones for their band are ticked for you).
  4. Leave Email them a welcome ticked and click Save. They can sign in to the app straight away.
  5. Send their contract: next step.

✍A contract to sign

  1. Documents › + Send a document to sign (or People › their name › Send them a document to sign).
  2. Choose the person, a title and the PDF. Leave SAADI counter-signs ticked and pick who. Click Send.
  3. They read and sign it on their phone. You're emailed: click Counter-sign, open it, sign, tick and Sign.
  4. Everyone gets the finished PDF with a signing certificate. Reminders go out automatically if it's not signed.

📄A new or updated policy

  1. Documents › Company documents › + Upload a document (or New version on an existing one).
  2. Tick Tell all staff now. Everyone gets an alert and ticks "I've read and understood".
  3. Who's read it shows who hasn't.

📅A new term

  1. Rota › New term…. Give it a name, the first Monday, number of weeks and the half-term Monday.
  2. Every class, time and usual coach is copied. Then change only what's different.

Month end (payroll)

  1. Open Payroll. It shows last month until the 10th, then this month.
  2. Tick Only ones to check and go through the flags: over holiday allowance, SSP sick days, undecided holiday, leavers, new starters.
  3. Click a person to see their month day by day. Add Extra hours for anything not on the rota (training, events, admin) with a note.
  4. Click ⬇ For QuickBooks (CSV): one "Hourly Pay" line per hourly person (worked + extra + holiday hours), with notes of holiday and SSP dates.
  5. In QuickBooks Advanced Payroll, enter or import the hours; enter sickness as leave so it works out SSP.
  6. Back in Payroll, click Mark as sent to payroll.
Salaried staff show "salary": they're not in the hours, but their holiday and sick days still show.

For staff (send them this)

  1. On your phone, open hub.saadigymnastics.co.uk/team.
  2. Type the email SAADI has for you and tap Email me a code. Type the 6-digit code from the email.
  3. Add it to your Home Screen (iPhone: Share › Add to Home Screen; Android: ⋮ › Add to Home screen). Open it from there.
  4. Tap Turn on notifications so you hear about holiday, cover and documents.
  5. My shifts: your next two weeks. Add these to my calendar puts them in your phone's calendar.
  6. Request holiday: pick the dates, add a note (within 3 weeks? say who's covering), Send request.
  7. I'm not well: tell us as early as you can. Only the owners see what's wrong.
  8. Shifts up for grabs (orange): tap I can do this on any you can cover. A manager confirms who gets it.
  9. Documents: read each one and tap I've read and understood. Contracts: read, type your name, sign with your finger, Sign.
