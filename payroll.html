Payroll

This page is for managers

Your own shifts and holiday are in the SAADI Team app.

Hours worked come from the rota (after every change and cover), less any day they were on holiday, on leave or off sick. Holiday is what Breathe deducted, split across months, checked against their allowance. Sickness lists the days they were due to work, for SSP. QuickBooks Advanced Payroll applies the pay rates and works out SSP.
