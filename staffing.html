Wages: hourly (non-salaried) staff Draft: amounts not agreed yet

For hourly-paid staff only. Salaried staff aren't on this pay scale and are left off the lists below.

Each pay band is a set amount on top of the starting rate for the person's age. SAADI sets the starting rates for 14 to 17 (there's no legal minimum under 16, and 16–17 never go below it); 18–20 and 21+ follow the minimum wage, so they go up by themselves each April.

Cost of the new pay scale

For a year from the date shown: each person's new pay scale (pay band + top-up, at their age each month) minus what payroll pays them now, times their weekly hours and the weeks worked. Nobody's pay goes down, so a lower rate counts as £0. Uses saved pay bands, pay and hours (press Save pay bands and pay first). Wages only: no employer NI or pension. People aged 22 and over aren't counted, as their pay isn't kept here.

39 = term time

Holiday entitlement

The same people. Standard is the legal minimum of 5.6 weeks a year: 5.6 × their usual hours per week. From overtime adds 12.07% of any hours worked beyond their pattern this leave year (the government's holiday rate for irregular hours, 5.6 ÷ 46.4 weeks). All figures are in hours. People who started part-way through the leave year get a share of the standard amount by start date.
