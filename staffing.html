Wages Draft: amounts not agreed yet

Each level is paid a set amount above the legal minimum wage for the person's age, so every rate goes up by itself when the minimum wage rises each April. Under-16s have no legal minimum, so they're paid the 16–17 rate.

Pay scale

Minimum wage rates used (gov.uk)

Add next year's rates when the government announces them (usually in the autumn Budget, starting 1 April).

Staff

Everyone in Breathe, with their date of birth from QuickBooks. Levels start from each person's positions (suggested) until you save. Rates use each person's age on the day.

Holiday entitlement

The same people. Standard is the legal minimum of 5.6 weeks a year: 5.6 × their usual hours per week. From overtime adds 12.07% of any hours worked beyond their pattern this leave year (the government's holiday rate for irregular hours, 5.6 ÷ 46.4 weeks). All figures are in hours. People who started part-way through the leave year get a share of the standard amount by start date.
