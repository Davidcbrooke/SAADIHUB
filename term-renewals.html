Reading the form responses…
To read the form responses, the reception@ mailer needs a small update (about 2 minutes, on the reception PC, in the browser signed in as reception@):
  1. Open the Enquiry Desk > Emails & settings and press Copy the mailer code (close the new Apps Script tab it opens).
  2. Go to script.google.com, open SAADI Enquiry Desk mailer, select all the code (Ctrl+A), paste (Ctrl+V) and save (Ctrl+S).
  3. Choose allowSending and press Run, then allow the permissions it asks for (sending email, the spreadsheet, filing emails and running every 5 minutes).
  4. Deploy > Manage deployments, click the pencil, set Version: New version, press Deploy. Then come back here and press Refresh responses.
Settings: response spreadsheet and renewal closing date

Until renewals close, places in next term's classes are held for children who haven't answered yet, so class changes are only offered where there's still a free place after that. From the closing date, those places are released.

Matched to next term's classes on the requested days and times, for the child's school year (worked out from their current class and their age in JustGo), not by colour, as colours cover different years in different classes. Space now: free after holding places for children who haven't renewed yet, so you can send the booking link now. Wait: the class only has room once renewals close. Full: no room even then. Families to remind

One email per family, from reception@, with a button to book each child's matching class for next term, and two buttons to tell us they want a different class or aren't continuing (these come to reception@ as emails). When a family tells you, press Changing class or Not continuing on their card so they aren't reminded again. Families without an email address in JustGo can't be selected.

Email preview

Change the wording of the first reminder

Use {parentName}, {childNames}, {place}, {spot}, {nextTerm}, {nextTermShort}, {startDate}, {classes} (where the booking buttons go) and {otherOptions} (the "different class / not continuing" buttons).

Families to call

Ring each family, then press what happened. Send link: their class emails the booking link for next term's matching class straight away. Send link: different class lets you pick another class for their school year, then emails that link. Leaving takes them off the list and out of the figures. No answer keeps them on the list so someone can try again. Remove from list takes them off the call list only (no email, figures unchanged); untick "Hide families already sorted" and press Put back on list to undo. Every call is saved with who rang and when, so nobody calls twice. Phone numbers come from JustGo with the morning sync. Call notes are deleted automatically after 6 months.
