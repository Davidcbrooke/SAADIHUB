Reading the form responses…
To read the form responses, the reception@ mailer needs a small update (about 2 minutes, on the reception PC, in the browser signed in as reception@):
  1. Open the Enquiry Desk > Emails & settings and press Copy the mailer code (close the new Apps Script tab it opens).
  2. Go to script.google.com, open SAADI Enquiry Desk mailer, select all the code (Ctrl+A), paste (Ctrl+V) and save (Ctrl+S).
  3. Choose allowSending and press Run, then allow the new permission (to read spreadsheets).
  4. Deploy > Manage deployments, click the pencil, set Version: New version, press Deploy. Then come back here and press Refresh responses.
Settings: response spreadsheet and renewal closing date

Until renewals close, places in next term's classes are held for children who haven't answered yet, so class changes are only offered where there's still a free place after that. From the closing date, those places are released.

Remind families who haven't booked

One email per family, from reception@, with a button to book each child's matching class for next term, and two buttons to tell us they want a different class or aren't continuing (these come to reception@ as emails). When a family tells you, press Changing class or Not continuing on their card so they aren't reminded again. Families without an email address in JustGo can't be selected.
